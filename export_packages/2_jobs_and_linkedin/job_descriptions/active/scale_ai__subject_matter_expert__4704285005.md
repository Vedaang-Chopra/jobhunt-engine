&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Subject Matter Expert Role Description&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong data-start=&quot;0&quot; data-end=&quot;39&quot; data-is-only-node=&quot;&quot;&gt;Engagement Type: Part-time (Hourly)&lt;/strong&gt; — Work as many or as few hours as you&#39;d like, with the flexibility to set your own schedule based on your availability.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Overview&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Subject Matter Expert is a specialized role focused exclusively on quality evaluation and standards definition within assigned projects. This role is designed for subject-matter experts who provide high-value insights through audits, quality frameworks, and performance analysis.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Subject Matter Expert does not engage in contributor management, real-time support, or operational task coordination. Instead, the role centers on assessing output quality, identifying systemic issues, and recommending improvements to ensure high standards are consistently met.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Key Responsibilities&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Quality Audits &amp;amp; Evaluation&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Conduct structured audits of task outputs to evaluate accuracy, consistency, and adherence to project guidelines.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Apply domain expertise to assess nuanced or complex work requiring specialized knowledge.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Identify patterns of quality issues and flag systemic gaps impacting output standards.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Quality Standards &amp;amp; Benchmarking&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Define and refine quality benchmarks, scoring rubrics, and evaluation criteria based on project needs.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Contribute to the development of gold standards and reference materials for high-quality outputs.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ensure quality frameworks reflect domain-specific rigor where applicable.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Analysis &amp;amp; Insights&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Analyze audit results to generate actionable insights on quality trends and performance gaps.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Provide structured reports with clear recommendations to improve overall output quality.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Partner with internal stakeholders to calibrate on quality expectations and scoring consistency.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Process Improvement (Quality-Focused)&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Recommend improvements to workflows, guidelines, and evaluation processes based on audit findings.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support calibration efforts to ensure alignment across reviewers and quality stakeholders.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives strictly related to quality outcomes.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Out of Scope (Role Boundaries)&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;No direct interaction with contributors (e.g., no office hours, Slack support, or real-time guidance).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;No task assignment, queue management, or operational oversight.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;No people management or performance management responsibilities.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Qualifications&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Deep domain expertise in a specialized field (e.g., medicine, law, engineering, etc.).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong analytical skills with the ability to evaluate complex outputs against defined standards.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience in quality assurance, auditing, or evaluation frameworks is preferred.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ability to produce clear, structured, and actionable feedback.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;High attention to detail and commitment to accuracy and consistency.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Engagement Expectations&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hourly, project-based work aligned to audit volume and quality needs.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Deliverables include completed audits, calibration input, and quality reports.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Operates independently with minimal oversight, focusing on expertise-driven contributions.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
