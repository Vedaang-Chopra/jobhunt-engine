&lt;h5&gt;&lt;strong style=&quot;font-size: 14px;&quot;&gt;Who You Are:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h5&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You are a seasoned full-stack engineer who thrives in greenfield environments where you have broad scope and the&amp;nbsp; opportunity to wear multiple hats. You excel in building impactful, AI-powered solutions and take pride in delivering intuitive, high-performance user experiences. In this role, you&#39;ll work closely with UX designers, AI/ML experts, and research scientists, both internally and at partner institutions, to create seamless interfaces that empower users to&amp;nbsp; interact effectively with our AI systems. You&#39;re energized by the opportunity to collaborate directly with researchers&amp;nbsp; to translate their needs into production applications that advance real scientific discovery.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Who We Are:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The Agentic Applications team builds open, production-grade AI systems that power scientific discovery. We focus on developing intelligent agents that help researchers explore literature, synthesize knowledge, and accelerate their work across real-world scientific use cases. The team combines strong engineering practices with close collaboration across Ai2&#39;s product and research orgs to deliver tools used by millions of researchers and developers worldwide.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Your Next Challenge:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The essential functions include, but are not limited to the following:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work closely with UX designers, product managers, and research scientists to develop rich UI experiences using React and robust backend services in Python&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Engage in the entire software development lifecycle, from ideation and design to implementation and testing to deployment and operational support, with a strong sense of ownership for results&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Scale our systems in a cost-effective and sustainable manner and be a driving force behind continuous improvements in reliability and automation&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Improve team velocity through contributions to software design, feature development, platform and framework enhancements, capacity planning, and deployment tooling&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Help set and maintain best practices in how we measure, monitor, and respond to system availability, latency, and general application health&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Communicate effectively across engineering, product, and research teams to align priorities and drive projects to completion&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Mentor less-senior engineers on the team in both technical and non-technical skills&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You’ll Need:&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Bachelor&#39;s degree and 8+ years of technical experience; relevant experience may substitute for education&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Professional experience with web development using JavaScript, HTML, and CSS&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong Python engineering skills&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Proficiency with front-end frameworks such as React&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience with AWS, Google Cloud, or similar cloud platforms&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience with analytics and observability tools like Heap, Datadog, or similar for monitoring performance, system health, and user interactions&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Solid understanding of integrating AI-powered capabilities into applications, including prompt engineering, memory management, agent workflows, and external tool integrations&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong communication skills and a sense of ownership for results&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
