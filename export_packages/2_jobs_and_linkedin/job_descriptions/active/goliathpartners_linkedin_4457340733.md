## Full Job Description Text

We’re partnering with a rapidly scaling AI company developing intelligent systems that automate sophisticated, high-value processes for enterprise customers. This role sits at the core of the company’s AI platform, with a focus on agent infrastructure, LLM systems, model orchestration, evaluation, and production-grade AI engineering.


You’ll operate at the intersection of research and engineering, taking new developments in AI and transforming them into dependable systems that can reason through complex tasks, interact with software environments, and deliver measurable results in production.


What You’ll Do
- Architect and build the infrastructure powering autonomous AI agents, including execution, tool use, memory, state, and computer interaction.
- Develop agent systems capable of navigating and completing complex, multi-stage workflows with minimal human intervention.
- Create model-selection and routing systems that dynamically determine the best model, strategy, or execution path for a given task.
- Design abstractions that support multiple foundation models and providers as the underlying AI ecosystem continues to evolve.
- Build evaluation platforms that continuously measure agent quality, reliability, and performance across production use cases.
- Establish testing methodologies and benchmarks for open-ended agent behavior, multi-step reasoning, and real-world task execution.
- Identify promising developments in LLMs, agent frameworks, tool use, and computer-use technology and turn them into scalable product capabilities.
- Rapidly prototype and iterate on prompting, retrieval, fine-tuning, model selection, and agent strategies to improve outcomes.
- Partner closely with engineering and leadership to influence architecture, technical priorities, and the continued growth of the AI organization.



What We’re Looking For
- Strong foundations in computer science, with a Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline preferred.
- Track record of building and deploying LLM applications, AI agents, intelligent automation, or similar AI-driven systems in production.
- Strong knowledge of agent architectures, LLM APIs, tool calling, retrieval, prompting, model routing, and orchestration.
- Experience working in an environment where AI capabilities and best practices are evolving rapidly.
- Ability to bridge research and engineering—evaluating new ideas, determining what is practical, and turning them into robust production systems.
- Strong ownership mentality and comfort operating in a fast-moving, highly technical environment.



Compensation & Benefits
- Base Salary: $220,000 – $275,000, depending on experience
- Location: San Francisco, CA, Hybrid
- Significant opportunity to influence the architecture, product, and technical direction of a rapidly growing AI company



Please only apply if you meet the core technical requirements outlined above.
