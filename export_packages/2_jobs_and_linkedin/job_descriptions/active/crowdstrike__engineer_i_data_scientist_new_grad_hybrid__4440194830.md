About The Role:

CrowdStrike is seeking a highly motivated new graduate to join our NGSIEM Agentic AI team. In this role, you will help build intelligent systems that analyze security data at massive scale. You will contribute to machine learning models, ranking and retrieval systems that power next-generation security operations. Working alongside experienced engineers, researchers, and security experts, you will help transform cutting-edge AI research into production-grade capabilities. This is a unique opportunity to solve challenging real-world problems at the intersection of AI, large-scale systems, and cybersecurity while making a direct impact on protecting organizations worldwide.

This is a hybrid role based out of our office in Sunnyvale, CA, requiring 2 days per week on-site.

What You’ll Do: 

- Develop, test, and deploy models that directly impact CrowdStrike products.
- Deploy machine learning techniques to develop highly accurate models
- Solve complex technical problems with a high degree of ownership and autonomy.
- Apply machine learning and AI technologies to improve product capabilities and user experiences.
- Develop automated tests and contribute to overall software quality and reliability.
- Participate actively in architecture, design, and code reviews.
- Continuously learn new technologies, industry trends, and emerging research relevant to AI and cybersecurity.
- Work on GenAI and LLM projects, including fine-tuning models, creating embedding-based search systems, and developing AI-driven prototypes to enhance business operations.
- Contribute ideas and innovations in a fast-paced, customer-focused environment.
- Collaborate with engineers, data scientists, and product teams to build machine learning models and AI-powered capabilities.
- Write production-ready code that can be relied on for real-time decision making by CrowdStrike products
- Achieve success by researching / developing through iteration and inventive feature engineering.
- Opportunity to work with technologies including, but not limited to Python, Go, Blob storage (S3), PyTorch, scikit-learn and SQL



What You’ll Need: 

- Advanced degree (MS or PhD) in a quantitative field (e.g., Statistics, Computer Science, Data Science, AI).
- Strong foundation in machine learning, statistics, or data science
- Hands-on school or internship experience with:
- building GenAI-focused application and applying LLMs and agentic AI (e.g., agents, reasoning workflows, or RAG)
- Have personally implemented models in common Deep Learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, and tools such as scikit-learn, and forecasting libraries.

- Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities, Machine Learning Concepts
- Experience with Go, Python and common data science tools through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects
- Demonstrated ability to analyze complex problems and build data-driven solutions
- Strong communication skills and ability to explain technical ideas clearly
- Ability to write clean, maintainable code
- Strong attention to detail and curiosity about security problems
- Can work collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
- Proven experience utilizing AI technologies to enhance decision-making, streamline workflows and processes, improve efficiency and drive business outcomes.
