## Full Job Description Text

We're looking for an AI-Powered Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) to help organizations transform business challenges into practical, scalable AI solutions. This is a unique opportunity for a seasoned software, cloud, or integration engineer who has expanded into Generative AI and wants to build production-ready solutions that deliver measurable business impact.
As a key member of our growing AI Center of Excellence, you'll work closely with business leaders, architects, engineers, cybersecurity teams, and product stakeholders to identify high-value AI opportunities and bring them to life. If you're passionate about solving real-world problems with AI and enjoy working at the intersection of business strategy and technology execution, we'd love to connect.
What You'll Do
- Partner directly with business stakeholders to identify and prioritize AI use cases with strong business value.
- Design, build, deploy, and operationalize enterprise-grade AI solutions from concept to production.
- Develop secure, scalable AI-powered applications using modern cloud-native architectures.
- Create AI-enabled integrations across enterprise platforms and business systems.
- Implement Generative AI solutions leveraging LLMs, RAG architectures, prompt engineering, agentic workflows, and evaluation frameworks.
- Build APIs, microservices, and cloud services that support AI and automation workloads.
- Collaborate with enterprise architecture, DevSecOps, engineering, integration, cybersecurity, product, and data teams.
- Establish best practices for AI architecture, governance, observability, security, and deployment.
- Implement CI/CD pipelines and automation to accelerate solution delivery.
- Monitor performance, optimize outcomes, troubleshoot issues, and continuously improve deployed AI applications.
- Produce technical documentation, architectural artifacts, and operational runbooks.
- Contribute to the growth of the AI Center of Excellence through knowledge sharing, mentoring, and standards development.

What We're Looking For
Required Qualifications
- 9+ years of experience in software engineering, cloud engineering, platform engineering, or enterprise integration development.
- Recent hands-on experience delivering enterprise AI or Generative AI solutions.
- Strong understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs) and modern AI architecture patterns.
- Experience with:
- Prompt Engineering
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- AI Evaluation and Testing Frameworks
- AI Orchestration and Agent Frameworks
- Cloud-native development experience with Azure, AWS, and/or GCP.
- Strong programming skills in:
- Python
- Java
- JavaScript
- Experience designing and developing REST APIs and microservices.
- Experience with Docker, containers, and Kubernetes.
- Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
- Strong understanding of enterprise integration patterns and distributed systems.
- Knowledge of authentication, authorization, identity management, and enterprise security controls.
- Excellent communication skills with the ability to engage both technical and business stakeholders.
- Strong analytical, troubleshooting, and problem-solving capabilities.
