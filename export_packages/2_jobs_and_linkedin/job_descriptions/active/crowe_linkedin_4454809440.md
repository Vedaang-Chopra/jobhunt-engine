Job Description

About the Role 

- As an AI Engineering team member, you will be instrumental in advancing new features and/or solutions from the Proof of Concept stage to full production readiness. Your role involves refining and enhancing AI solutions to ensure they are robust, scalable, and aligned with business objectives. 
- Execute on the technical vision for development of AI solutions and delivery of AI features to ensure they are scalable and production-ready. 
- Refine user stories and define clear acceptance criteria for feature requests to ensure alignment with business needs. 
- Write high-quality, production-ready code and assist in code reviews to maintain standards of excellence. 
- Develop and implement comprehensive testing protocols, including smoke tests and unit tests, to ensure solution reliability. 
- Support the debugging process and issue triage to maintain solution integrity throughout the development cycle. 
- Participate in ethical reviews and risk/security evaluations to uphold Crowe’s standards for responsible AI deployment. 



Required Skills

- Expertise in Python programming and experience with version control systems like Git. 
- Strong ability to work independently and execute tasks efficiently in a dynamic environment. 
- Proficiency in navigating project ambiguities and iterative testing processes to refine AI solutions. 
- Excellent communication skills, capable of effectively documenting and presenting technical details to nontechnical stakeholders. 



Preferred Skills

- Solid understanding of how to align technical outputs with business requirements. 
- Knowledge of prompt engineering and retrieval augmented generation (RAG) and experience with advanced AI tools such as ChatGPT Enterprise and Microsoft Copilot, Azure AI Foundry. 
- Experience in fine-tuning large language models (LLMs). 
- Previous experience in professional services or a similar industry, with an understanding of the unique challenges and opportunities it presents.
