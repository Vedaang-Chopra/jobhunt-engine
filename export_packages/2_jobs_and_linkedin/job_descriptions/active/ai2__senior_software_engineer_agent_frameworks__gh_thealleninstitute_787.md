Who You Are:

The Allen Institute for AI (Ai2) is hiring a Senior Engineer to build the foundational architecture behind AI research agents that explore and reason over scholarly literature. You'll design the systems that define how agents, tools, and services interact, creating SDKs, APIs, and execution frameworks that enable researchers to build, evaluate, and scale intelligent systems. You'll contribute to the agent architecture, build infrastructure for sandboxed execution and benchmarking, and create evaluation frameworks that measure component&nbsp; performance. You're a seasoned engineer who thrives in greenfield environments, excels at building robust developer-facing infrastructure, and is energized by making complex distributed systems feel simple.

Who We Are:&nbsp;

The Agentic Applications team builds open, production-grade AI systems that power scientific discovery. We focus on developing intelligent agents that help researchers explore literature, synthesize knowledge, and accelerate their work across real-world scientific use cases. The team combines strong engineering practices with close collaboration across Ai2's product and research orgs to deliver tools used by millions of researchers and developers worldwide.

Your Next Challenge:

The essential functions include, but are not limited to the following:


Design and build the frameworks, SDKs, and execution surfaces that define how agents, tools, and services interact across our research platform

Build infrastructure for sandboxed agent execution, enabling researchers to safely run, inspect, and iterate on agent workflows at scale

Design and maintain evaluation and benchmarking frameworks that measure the performance of individual agent components and end-to-end systems

Develop the abstractions and developer-facing tooling that let research scientists build, compose, and deploy agents without owning the underlying infrastructure

Drive continuous improvements in reliability, observability, and cost-efficiency across the systems agents run in and depend on

Engage in the entire software development lifecycle, from ideation and design to implementation and testing to deployment and operational support, with a strong sense of ownership for results

Collaborate across engineering and research teams to ensure maintainability, test coverage, and robust deployment

Communicate effectively across engineering, product, and research teams to align priorities and drive projects to completion



What You’ll Need:


Bachelor's degree and 8+ years of technical experience; relevant experience may substitute for education

Strong Python engineering skills, with experience building production services and developer-facing tools

Experience designing SDKs, APIs, or frameworks that other engineers built on top of and adopted

Hands-on experience building or operating agentic systems – agent orchestration, tool integration, sandboxed execution, memory management, or the surfaces agents run in and consume

Experience designing evaluation or benchmarking infrastructure that measures system or component performance

Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or similar), containerization, and deployment automation

Strong communication skills and a sense of ownership for results
