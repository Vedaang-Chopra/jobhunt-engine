# Outreach and Application Rules

## Choose the right ask
Possible actions: direct referral request, introduction request, role-specific question, informational conversation, hiring-manager message, recruiter outreach, reply to hiring post, or connection request first.

## Message quality
Use real shared context: university, mutual, employer overlap, technical domain, research/project overlap, specific role/team, or hiring post. Do not send generic networking spam.

## Referral logic
Do not ask every employee for referrals. Prefer people who know the candidate, share meaningful context, are relevant to the team, are credible internal advocates, or have a realistic reason to respond.

## Contact discovery
Use legitimate public/professional sources such as company pages, professional websites, GitHub, papers, conferences, university/lab pages, and reputable professional contact sources.

Every email must be labelled as public/verified, verified via reliable source, inferred/unverified, or unavailable. Never present an invented address as fact.

## Human approval boundary
Unless standing permission exists, Hermes may autonomously research, browse, analyze, rank, prepare outreach, and prepare/fill draft application data.

Ask before sending LinkedIn messages, sending emails, submitting applications, materially changing public profiles, or deleting non-obviously redundant information.

## Application help
Open the correct official application, fill known profile facts, use browser autofill/extensions where available, identify missing fields, ask for unresolved answers, and maintain application status. Never invent application answers.
