# Profile and Preferences Rules

## Goal
All workers must use one canonical candidate profile. Do not independently infer or recreate profile facts.

## Target role titles
Research Engineer, Research Scientist, Applied Scientist, ML Engineer, AI Engineer, Software Engineer–ML/AI, ML Systems/Inference Engineer, Applied AI Engineer, and closely related roles.

## Classify actual work separately from title
Possible domains include Agentic AI, reasoning, post-training/RL, LLM training, inference/serving, RAG/retrieval, multimodal/VLM, general MLE, AI/ML security, robotics/autonomy, CAD/design/manufacturing AI, and coding agents/developer tools.

Read the JD. Never infer domain from title alone.

## Geography
Primary market: United States.

Default preference:
1. San Francisco Bay Area / California.
2. Seattle and other major U.S. AI hubs.
3. Other strong U.S. opportunities.
4. Remote U.S.
5. Very strong India roles.
6. Exceptional roles elsewhere.

Location is a ranking factor, not a hard exclusion unless explicitly stated.

## Comparative advantages
When evaluating roles/companies, explicitly consider prior employer/competitor adjacency, Georgia Tech/alumni links, lab relationships, CAD/design research, security/AI background, agentic/reasoning work, relevant projects, former colleagues, and domain overlap.

## Never invent
Work authorization, years of experience, publications, degrees, skills, relocation preference, compensation, or other eligibility facts. Ask when material information is missing.
