## Seniority rule (revised per user direction, 2026-08-23)

Posted year bars are wish-lists; designations vary by company (startups especially).
The hard exclude is therefore **designation-based**: staff / principal / director /
VP / manager, or an explicit **9+ years** stated bar. "Senior" titles and 5–8y
bars are kept with a soft flag — user's effective experience is ~5–6 years
counting internships. Under this rule 5 previously-excluded rows stay eligible
(1 direct recovery; the rest were already excluded for other reasons).

## Systemic gaps this audit exposed

1. Ingest has no hard filters for seniority/location/visa → noise accumulates silently.
2. `seniority` field is free-text soup (18 distinct values incl. "6+ yrs", "Mid-level", blanks) — should be normalized enum per SCHEMA.md.
3. Keyword scorer rewards title/JD overlap even when the role type is wrong (GTM Analytics scored 49.4).
4. Visa availability should be a schema column, not notes text.
